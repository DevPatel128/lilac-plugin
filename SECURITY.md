# Security

> **Plain-language starting point, not legal advice; have a qualified lawyer review it before relying on it.**

## Report a vulnerability
Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

1. Email **devpatel1286@gmail.com** (Dev Patel) with what you found, the version, and steps to reproduce.
2. Do not include real secrets or customer data. Describe them instead.
3. Wait for our reply before you share the details anywhere.

## What to expect
We reply within **7 days** (one maintainer; best effort). We will tell you whether we accept the report, and we will credit you if you want that.

## Supported versions
Only the latest release gets fixes. Please reproduce the problem on the latest release first.

## Scope
This plugin: its skills, agents, hooks and scripts. Problems in Claude Code itself go to Anthropic.

## Out of scope
- Problems in sites you built with Lilac: those are yours to fix.
- Third-party services and tools (hosting, email, payments, AI tools). Report them to their owners.
- Denial of service, spam, social engineering or physical attacks.
- Reports with no steps to reproduce, or automated scanner output with no analysis.

## Safe harbour
We will not take legal action against good-faith research that follows this policy. Good faith means: you stay inside the scope above, you do not access or change other people's data, you do not disrupt services, and you give us time to fix the problem before you share it. This is the maintainer's statement of intent. It cannot bind other people, such as third-party services.

## Disclosure timeline
We reply within 7 days. We aim to give a fix or a plan within 90 days of your report. After that, or after a fix ships, you may share the details. If we need longer, we will say why.

## No bug bounty
There is no bug bounty. We cannot pay for reports. We can credit you if you want that.
