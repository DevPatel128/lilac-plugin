---
id: UX-0027
title: Radios for one, checkboxes for many, no preselection
kind: pattern
domain: forms
claim: Control type signals whether one or several answers are allowed, and preselected answers get missed.
guidance: Use radios for one choice and checkboxes for several; add None of the above where valid; do not preselect an answer people must decide.
evidence: GOV.UK Design System, Radios; https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/radios/; read 2026-11-21; figure: avoid pre-selecting; inline radios only for two short options
confidence: low
applies_to: [website, webapp]
automated_check: manual
edges: [UX-0086]
---
Note: single source; treat as a prompt to test, not a rule.
