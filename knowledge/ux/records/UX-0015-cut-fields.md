title: Fewer fields, higher completion
kind: principle
domain: forms
claim: Every removed field makes a form faster, and most checkouts need fewer fields than they show.
guidance: Remove fields you can derive or collect later. Ask for one full-name field, hide the second address line and coupon behind links, and delay account creation until after the purchase.
