---
id: UX-0001
title: Hidden navigation costs discovery
kind: principle
domain: navigation
claim: Menus hidden behind an icon are used less and hide content from visitors compared with visible navigation.
guidance: Show the main links as visible labelled items when there are few; keep a hidden menu only for long lists, and add other visible routes (links in content, search) beside it.
evidence: Nielsen Norman Group, Hamburger Menus and Hidden Navigation; https://www.nngroup.com/articles/hamburger-menus/; read 2026-11-21; figure: 179 participants, 6 sites; desktop use 27% hidden vs 48% visible; content discoverability down by more than 20%; mobile use 57% hidden vs 86% combo
confidence: medium
applies_to: [website, webapp, mobile]
automated_check: persona-test:completion
edges: [UX-0002, UX-0012]
