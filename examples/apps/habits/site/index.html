    About and privacy
    
    
    Fictional sample. Pocket Habits is an example web app made for the Lilac framework. It is not a real product or service. Names and starter habits are invented.
    
    
    What it is
    A small habit tracker. You add habits, tick the days you did them, and see streaks and weekly progress. Version , data format .
    
    
    What is stored
    Your habits, the days you ticked, and your settings, in this browser's local storage. A saved copy of the app files is kept by the browser so it works offline.
    
    
    What is not collected
    No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no advertising, no network requests to other sites, and no copy of your data on any server. The app has no backend.
    
    
    Your control
    Export a backup, import one, or erase everything from Settings. Clearing this site's data in your browser also erases it.
    
    
    Limits
    Anyone who can use this browser profile can see and change the data. Clearing browser data without exporting loses your habits. This is a demo and makes no claim about health or results.
