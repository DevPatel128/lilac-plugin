What this demo shows

A static site with a filterable product list, a feeding guide, an ingredients page and a contact form that checks your input. It runs from plain files, with no framework and no outside requests.

What it leaves out on purpose

No reviews, ratings, stock counts, health claims or regulatory wording. Those would need real evidence from a real company, and this one does not exist.

Biscuit & Bowl is a fictional sample brand. This site is a demo made with Lilac's website track. No real products, prices or orders exist, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Prices are in sample currency (BB$). Ingredient lists are sample text.
