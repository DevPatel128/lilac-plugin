Questions, answered briefly

Is Biscuit & Bowl a real company?

No. It is a fictional sample brand used to show what Lilac's website track can build.

Can I buy anything?

No. There is no cart, no checkout and no stock. The plan builder only adds up sample prices.

What currency are the prices in?

Sample currency, written BB$. It has no real value.

Are the ingredient lists real?

No. They are sample text so the page layout can be shown.

Where does my contact form message go?

Nowhere. The form checks your input in the browser and then says nothing was sent.

Does the site work without JavaScript?

Yes for reading and navigation. The product filter, plan builder and form check need JavaScript.
