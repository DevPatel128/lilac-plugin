Ingredients, in plain words

What the words on a sample bag mean. We describe what each thing is, not what it does for a dog.

The sample list, grouped

Chicken, turkey, lamb
The main protein in the sample recipes. Each product lists only one.
Rice, brown rice, oats, barley
Cooked cereal grains that give the recipes their bulk.
Peas, pumpkin, sweet potato, carrot, apple
Vegetables and fruit, cooked and mixed in.
Sunflower oil, flaxseed
Plant oils and seeds added in small amounts.
Dried parsley, mint, rosemary, thyme
Dried herbs used for flavour in the sample recipes.

What this page does not say

It makes no health claims, no vet claims and no statements about nutrition rules. A real brand would need its recipes checked and its wording approved before saying more.
