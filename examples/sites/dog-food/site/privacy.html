Privacy (demo)

This is a fictional demo, so Biscuit & Bowl collects no data. The form sends nothing: it does not post to a server, and the name, email and message you type stay in your browser and are not put in the address. Only plain choices such as a day, a class or a party size can appear in the address of the thank-you page, in your own browser history. There are no cookies, no analytics and no requests to other sites. A real site would say here what it keeps, why, for how long, and how to ask for deletion.
