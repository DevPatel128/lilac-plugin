How it works: three steps

A sample guide, written as placeholder text for a fictional product.

  1. Pick a life stage and a bag

    Choose puppy, adult or senior, then a bag size that suits how many dogs you feed. The plan builder lets you try combinations.

  2. Measure and split into meals

    Use a level scoop and split the day's amount into two meals. The scoop size and amounts on a real product would come from its maker, so this demo gives no numbers.

  3. Switch over slowly

    When changing foods, many owners mix the old and new over about a week. In a real shop this step would link to the maker's own transition notes.

This guide is sample text. For feeding advice about a real dog, ask your vet.
