Approach

Five steps. Each ends with a sheet you can hold.

A sample process for an imaginary studio. Timings, fees and deliverables are not stated because none are real.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We start with how you live or work, not with a style. SAMPLE output: a one-page statement of needs, agreed in writing.

  2. 02

    Measure

    The site, the budget range and the rules that apply are listed before any line is drawn. SAMPLE output: a site and constraints sheet.

  3. 03

    Draw

    Plans first, then sections, then elevations. Each sheet answers one question. SAMPLE output: a numbered drawing set.

  4. 04

    Test

    Drawings are walked through room by room with you, then revised. SAMPLE output: a marked-up set and a change list.

  5. 05

    Hand over

    Final drawings, a materials list and a plain-language note on what happens next. SAMPLE output: a handover folder.

SAMPLE note: the loft plan shows what a finished sheet looks like at step 3, with the section line, the furniture and the poche of the walls drawn together.
