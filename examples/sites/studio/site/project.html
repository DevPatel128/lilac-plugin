Courtyard House

SAMPLE brief: a family house for an imaginary plot, organised around one square of open sky so that every room has two aspects.

Category
Residential
Type
Single-family house
Site
Sample plot, sample town
Status
Imaginary, never built
Area
SAMPLE: about 190 m²

The brief

SAMPLE text. The imagined clients wanted a quiet house that never feels shut in. The plan answers with a ring of rooms around a courtyard: kitchen and living on the south side, sleeping rooms to the north, a covered walk joining them.

SAMPLE text. Doors swing against walls, not into the room, so each room keeps its full width. The section line A-A on the plan cuts through the courtyard and both wings.
