Post 01 · SAMPLE

Why we draw the section first

SAMPLE text. A plan shows where things are. A section shows what it feels like to stand there: how high the ceiling is, where the light comes from, how far it is to the ground.

SAMPLE text. Drawing the section first makes those questions unavoidable, and the plan then has something to answer to.

Post 02 · SAMPLE

Notes on a courtyard

SAMPLE text. A courtyard is the cheapest way to give every room a second wall of light. It also gives the house somewhere to be quiet that is not a room.

SAMPLE text. The sample Courtyard House uses a single square, one tree and a floor of stone, and little else.
