# Funnel: Still Water (fictional demo)

Fictional demo site. There are no real visitors, customers or conversions. Nothing here is a measured result.

## Business goal
Request a sample trial class (demo). One goal: the visitor completes the demo form.

## Ideal customer
A person trying the demo (SAMPLE persona only). No real customer exists.

## Stages
### Aware
- Page or screen: index.html
- Visitor's question: what is this and what can I do here?
- Content that answers it: the home page headline and a button "Book a sample trial class".
- The one next action: "Book a sample trial class".
- Friction to remove: none known; unverified with real people.

### Interested
- Page or screen: the topic pages in the main menu
- Visitor's question: is this for me?
- Content that answers it: sample pages, all marked fictional.
- The one next action: a closing button on every page to the form.
- Friction to remove: pages without a next step (fixed: each ends with a link to the form).

### Considering
- Page or screen: the same pages
- Visitor's question: what will happen if I send the form?
- Content that answers it: the form page says nothing is sent and links the privacy note.
- The one next action: the form.
- Friction to remove: UNKNOWN until real people try it.

### Ready
- Page or screen: the form page
- Visitor's question: what do I have to fill in?
- Content that answers it: labelled fields (class, day and level are the only values that can appear in the address).
- The one next action: "Request sample trial class".
- Friction to remove: works with or without JavaScript.

### Done
- Page or screen: thanks.html (and an in-page message when JavaScript is on)
- Visitor's question: did it work?
- Content that answers it: "Demo submitted: thank you", a status line saying nothing was sent and nobody will reply.
- The one next action: back to the main page or the form.
- Friction to remove: none known.

### Return
- Page or screen: privacy.html and the contact link on every page
- Visitor's question: who do I ask?
- Content that answers it: no one: this is a fictional demo.
- The one next action: back to the main page.
- Friction to remove: not applicable.

## Main path
1. Home: "Book a sample trial class" (1 click to the form page). 2. Form page: "Request sample trial class" (submit, lands on thanks.html). Budget: 3 clicks.

## Secondary paths
- Main menu to any page, then the closing button to the form.
- Footer and menu links to the form page.

## Confirmation
thanks.html: "Demo submitted: thank you". A status line says this is a fictional demo, nothing was sent, nobody will reply, and what a real site would do next. With JavaScript on, the page shows the in-page confirmation instead.

## Follow-up
None. Nothing is sent, stored or replied to.

## Trust
Only true elements. Everything is marked fictional or SAMPLE. A privacy note is linked next to the form. No reviews, ratings, logos or counts.

## Measurement
off. No analytics, no cookies.
