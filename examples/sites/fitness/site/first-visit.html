First visit

Your first class, step by step

Plain steps for a first visit to this imagined studio.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothes you can stretch in
  • A water bottle
  • A small towel, if you like
  • Your own mat, or borrow one at the studio

Which class?

Choose Beginner or First Steps on Saturday. Tell the teacher it is your first time and mention anything you would like them to know.

On the day

  1. Arrive ten minutes early. There is time to say hello and find a spot.
  2. Sign in at the front desk. Say it is your first visit.
  3. Leave shoes and bags in the entrance. Bring only what you need into the room.
  4. Set up your mat. Pick any spot. The teacher will say where props are.
  5. Take the class at your own pace. Rest whenever you like. You can leave quietly if you need to.
  6. Say thanks and ask questions. The teacher will stay for a few minutes after class.
