Privacy

Privacy (demo)

This is a fictional demo, so Corner Kiln collects no data. The form sends nothing: it does not post to a server, and the name, email and message you type stay in your browser and are not put in the address. Only plain choices such as a day, a class or a party size can appear in the address of the thank-you page, in your own browser history. There are no cookies, no analytics and no requests to other sites. The only thing your browser may remember is whether you paused motion, in session storage; it never leaves your device. A real site would say here what it keeps, why, for how long, and how to ask for deletion.
