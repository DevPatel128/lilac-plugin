How teams might use it

Three illustrative examples.

These are made-up scenarios to show the product idea. They are not customers, results or case studies.

Example

A small design studio

Illustrative scenario. Not a customer story.

Who
A handful of designers sharing client work.
Goal
See which projects are waiting on feedback.
How they might use Tasklane
One board per client with lanes for Brief, In progress, Feedback and Done. The timeline shows overlapping deadlines.
What they might notice
Waiting work is easy to spot because it sits in its own lane.
Example

A support team

Illustrative scenario. Not a customer story.

Who
A few people handling requests that need follow-up.
Goal
Keep follow-ups from being forgotten.
How they might use Tasklane
A board with lanes for New, Investigating and Resolved. Comments hold the notes on each case.
What they might notice
Notes stay with the case instead of in separate messages.
Example

A volunteer committee

Illustrative scenario. Not a customer story.

Who
People organising a yearly community event in their spare time.
Goal
Share a plan without a long meeting.
How they might use Tasklane
A template board for the event, copied each year. Each task is a card with a date on the timeline.
What they might notice
Last year’s plan is a starting point instead of a blank page.
