What it would do

Three things, done plainly.

Boards you can read at a glance

Lanes for the stages your team already uses. Move a card and everyone sees the same state.

A timeline for what is next

See work laid out across days so a gap or a pile-up is visible before it becomes a problem.

Comments beside the work

Questions and decisions stay on the card they are about, not in a separate thread to hunt for.

How it would work

Capture, arrange, review.

  1. Capture

    Add work as short cards. Start from a blank board or a template.

  2. Arrange

    Drag cards between lanes and onto the timeline as plans change.

  3. Review

    Look back over finished work with your team and adjust the next round.
