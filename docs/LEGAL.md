# Legal files, in plain words

> **Plain-language starting point, not legal advice; have a qualified lawyer review it before relying on it.**

These files describe the terms. They do not make anyone immune from a claim or a law. They are not a substitute for a lawyer.

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `LICENSE` | MIT licence. You may use, copy, change and sell the plugin if you keep the notice. |
| `NOTICE` | Third-party skills included here, with their own licences. |

| `PRIVACY.md` | What stays on your machine, what the plugin does not do, how to delete it. |
| `SECURITY.md` | How to report a vulnerability, scope, safe harbour, no bug bounty. |

## The honest limits
- These files do not replace legal advice.
- They bind the plugin's authors and users as far as the law allows. They do not bind your visitors, your hosts or the courts.
- Lilac's checks are automated aids. They are not an audit or certification.
- Nothing here says the plugin or your site meets GDPR, CCPA or any other law.
- The name "Lilac" has had no trademark search. Availability is UNVERIFIED.

## Checklist if you ship a site made with Lilac
- [ ] Have a qualified person review your legal pages (privacy notice, terms, cookie notice if needed).
- [ ] Check that your name, logo and product names do not clash with someone else's trademark.
- [ ] Check the accessibility law that applies in your market. Automated checks are not enough.
- [ ] Do not copy other people's text, images or code without the right to.
- [ ] Get consent before you collect personal data. Say what you collect and why.
- [ ] Keep your own keys and passwords secret. Never put them in code or in chat.
- [ ] Read the terms of your hosting, email and payment services. The costs are yours.
- [ ] Review AI-generated content and code before you publish it.
- [ ] Test with real people. Simulated visitors are not real users.
