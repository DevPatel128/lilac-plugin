# Code of conduct

## Our standard
Be direct and be kind. Be blunt about work, not about people.

- Critique code, plans and evidence, never the person.
- Assume good faith, then check the facts.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Do not publish others' private information.

## Scope
Issues, pull requests, reviews and any space where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement
Report conduct problems privately to **devpatel1286@gmail.com** (Dev Patel). Reports are kept confidential and the reporter is never retaliated against.

Maintainers decide in this order, proportionate to the harm: a private note, a public correction, a temporary block, a permanent block. They may edit or remove comments and close threads that breach this code. A maintainer who is party to a report steps aside for it.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
