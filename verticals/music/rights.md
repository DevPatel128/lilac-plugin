# Rights (read before publishing audio)

- Playing a track needs two rights: the master (the recording) and the composition (the song). The owner must hold both, or hold licences for them. Lilac does not check this.
- Samples, covers and features each need their own clearance. List them in the brief. UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN.
- Embeds from Spotify, Apple Music, SoundCloud and Bandcamp are the safe default when rights are unclear: the platform carries the licence.
- Spotify preview URLs are no longer issued to new apps. Do not build on them.
- Mailing list: collect consent with a plain-language notice, confirm by email (double opt-in), and give a one-click unsubscribe. Covers GDPR and India's DPDP Act. This is a checklist, not legal advice.
- Tour data: Bandsintown needs written consent for an app_id. Never invent dates.
